Our Mission

Making a Lasting Impact Through Compassion, Education, and Health Advocacy.

Mission & Vision

Mission Statement

What better way to impact the health and well-being of our communities than to mentor youths in becoming advocates for health promotion and disease prevention?

Vision Statement

We strive to assist organizations in strengthening their capacity to provide services and to enhance the work environment with the objective of expanding the delivery of health education, health promotion, and harm reduction to at-risk groups.

Our Values

In keeping with its overall aim, Deskan Institute & Training, Inc., has continued to assist organizations in strengthening their capacity to provide services and to enhance the work environment with the objective of expanding the delivery of health education, health promotion, and harm reduction to at-risk groups. It is important for us to come together as communities to celebrate humanity, compassion, and understanding, and to exercise the power we all have to make a difference.
